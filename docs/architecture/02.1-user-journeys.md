# 2.1. User journeys

Getting to a document drive involves two people: a platform owner who prepares
the organization and its access, then a business user who works in an authorized
space. Signing in alone does not grant access to documents.

## From an empty platform to an organization's first user

On a new installation, the first platform owner signs in, accepts the terms of
use and activates ownership with the installation's bootstrap token. This is a
one-time setup.

```mermaid
flowchart TB
    owner["Activate platform ownership"] --> organization["Set up an organization"]
    organization --> access["Provision users, teams<br/>and access roles"]

    classDef default fill:#F1F5F9,stroke:#64748B,color:#0F172A
```

### A platform owner creates an organization

In **Organizations**, the owner enters a name and selects an organization owner
and administrator from the directory. Creating the organization does not itself
give those people access to team documents.

### Provisioning the business account

In **Provisioning**, the platform owner imports a prepared bundle of accounts,
organizations, teams and roles. This import requires a compatible Keycloak
installation. The result reports success or the cause of failure; completed
changes can remain after a failure.

The platform owner's area is for governance. The following journey uses Priya's
separate business account, with access already assigned to her team.

## Personal display choices

In Profile, each person chooses a theme, display mode and language. Fred saves
these choices with sidebar and list-sort preferences for reuse across browsers.
They load after sign-in, before application content appears. Without saved
preferences, Fred uses the platform theme, system display mode and browser
language. Pane widths and trace expansion remain specific to the browser.

## Priya opens Resources

Priya signs in and accepts the current terms of use. She selects an accessible
team from Home, then opens **Resources**, the document drive for that space.

```mermaid
flowchart TB
    login["Sign in and accept terms"] --> space["Choose an accessible space"]
    space --> drive["Open the document drive"]
    drive --> browse["Browse folders<br/>and documents"]
    drive --> create["Create a folder<br/>with write access"]

    classDef default fill:#F1F5F9,stroke:#64748B,color:#0F172A
```

The breadcrumb shows her current folder. To create `ArxivAi`, she enters its name
and confirms; the drive refreshes and the folder remains after reloading.
A duplicate name or missing write permission prevents creation and displays an
error. An administrator role alone does not grant content write access.

## Priya opens her personal space

If entitled, Priya can choose **Personal space** instead of a team. Fred creates
it on first opening and reuses it afterwards. It has its own content permissions;
organization and team roles do not automatically grant access to its contents.
