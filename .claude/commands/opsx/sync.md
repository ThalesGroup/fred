---
name: "OPSX: Sync"
description: "Synchronize approved delta specifications for a selected OpenSpec increment when explicitly requested. Does not restore the retired specification corpus."
---

Read and follow [the shared openspec-sync-specs skill](../../skills/openspec-sync-specs/SKILL.md).
Apply it to `$ARGUMENTS` within the user's selected increment and authorization.
The repository's `AGENTS.md` governs scope and validation; this command introduces
no separate workflow or automatic OpenSpec requirement.
