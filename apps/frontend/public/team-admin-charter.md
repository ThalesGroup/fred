# Team administrator charter

This is a template. A deployment replaces it with its own charter through its theme archive (`team-admin-charter.md` and `team-admin-charter.fr.md`).

As a team administrator, you commit to:

1. Keep your team limited to the people entitled to join it.
2. Review your team's usage regularly against your organization's rules.
3. Designate a backup administrator.
4. Delegate resource administration to the team's editors.
5. Check the quality of the documents your team ingests.

Your administrator rights are activated once you accept.
