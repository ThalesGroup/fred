# Notifications

The **bell** in the app bar, on the far left under the other entries, tells you
when something concerns you: an agent you published was changed by someone
else, or it left the marketplace. A number on the bell counts the notifications
you have not seen yet.

## Open your notifications

Click the bell: your notifications open beside the bar, newest first. Click
outside or press **Escape** to close them. On a small screen they take the
whole screen, with a back arrow.

## Seen and read

- **Seen**: opening the bell marks everything it shows as seen, and the number
  goes away. A notification that arrives while the list is open still counts
  on the bell afterwards.
- **Read**: each notification stays highlighted, with a dot, until you click
  it. Clicking it takes you where it points, usually the team's **Agents**
  page.

The bell refreshes on its own every few seconds while Fred is on screen; no
need to reload the page.

## Delete or clear

Hover a notification (or reach it with the keyboard) to show its cross, which
deletes it. **Clear all**, at the top, deletes every notification at once.
Deleting a notification only removes it from your list: it changes nothing on
the agent.

Notifications are deleted on their own **30 days** after they arrive.

## The marketplace notifications

Both concern an agent you published on the
[marketplace](/help/en/features/agents#the-agents-marketplace).

- **Published agent changed**: another member of the team saved a change to
  your agent, so the marketplace still shows the previous version. **Open the
  agent** takes you to the team's agents, where you can update the published
  version. Several changes in a row give a single notification. Once the agent
  is updated or removed from the marketplace, the notification goes away for
  whoever did it, and shows **Handled by** followed by their name for the
  others.
- **Agent removed from the marketplace**: a platform administrator removed your
  agent, or it was removed automatically (the team became private, publishing
  was turned off, the agent was deactivated or suspended). The notification
  gives the reason and the administrator's message if there is one. When
  someone of your team removes it themselves, nobody is notified.

> Only agents published or updated since notifications exist know who
> published them. An agent published earlier notifies nobody until someone
> updates its published version.
